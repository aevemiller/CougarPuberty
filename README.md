# Perimenopause and Menopause Guide

I wanted to understand all the new science on perimenopause and menopause but couldn't find a definitive place, with up to date research, and plain language that I could go to so, I built it with the help of my AI agent Claude (Opus 5.5). This is a plain-language guide to perimenopause and menopause for women, their friends and their families. It explains what changes in hormones, the brain and the body, and when, then lays out the treatments and habits with the strongest evidence. All research is linked to its source at the bottom of the page. 

Evidence is current to November 2026. The guide draws mainly on:

- American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists, Clinical Practice Guideline No. 13 (2026)
- The Menopause Society, hormone therapy (2022) and nonhormone therapy (2023) position statements
- European Society of Endocrinology guideline on menopause and perimenopause (2025)
- AUA/SUFU/AUGS guideline on genitourinary syndrome of menopause (2025)
- FDA labeling changes for menopausal hormone therapy (2025 to 2026)

This page is general information. It doesn't replace advice from a clinician who knows your health history.

## How the site works

The whole site is one file, `index.html`, with its styles, charts and scripts built in. GitHub Pages serves it from the `main` branch. The only outside resource is the Google Fonts stylesheet.

## Updating it

Edit `index.html` and commit to `main`. GitHub Pages republishes within a minute or two.
